About Me

I am Iza Loren B. Maro from Barangay Salag Siaton Negros Oriental. I am a friendly and responsible person who enjoys meeting new people. I always do my best in school and strive to improve myself every day.

I am an irregular student here at NORSU-SC because I was not able to pass the entrance exam when I first took it at NORSU-SC. I did not want to stop studying for one year, so my parents decided to enroll me at COSCA, a private Catholic school in Dumaguete City.

I took BS Criminology there, and honestly, it was a great experience for me. Being a cadet at COSCA gave me many memorable experiences I met new friends and learned many things along the way.

After some time, I decided to continue my studies at NORSU-SC. Now, I am already a 3rd-year regular BSITstudent here. Looking back, I am thankful for the experience I had at COSCA because it became an important part of my journey as a student.
